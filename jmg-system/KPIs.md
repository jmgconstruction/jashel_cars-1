# KPIs — Review Every Monday (15 minutes)

## Sales quality
- Leads received / qualified / declined
- Qualified → proposal → signed conversion rate (target: rising, not volume-chasing)
- Average contract value (target: rising)
- % of signed projects that were not price-led

## Delivery
- Pre-con checklist complete before mobilization (target 100%)
- Weekly client updates sent on time (target 100%)
- Change orders signed before work (target 100%)
- Schedule variance (days late / early)
- Budget variance (% vs. contract + approved changes)
- Punch list items at final walkthrough (target: falling)

## Client outcomes
- Client satisfaction score at closeout (1–10)
- Reviews and referrals received per completed project
- Warranty callbacks within 90 days (target: falling)

## Cash
- Deposits received before mobilization (100%)
- Invoices outstanding > 15 days
- Gross margin per project vs. estimate
