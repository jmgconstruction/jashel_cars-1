# Discovery Call Script (15–20 min)

**Open (warm, confident):**
"Thanks for making the time. My goal today is to understand your project and see whether we're a good fit. If we are, I'll explain how we work and what the next steps look like."

**Ask:**
1. Tell me about the project and what you want it to achieve.
2. What matters most — quality, timeline, design, or budget? How would you rank them?
3. Have you built or renovated before? What went well or poorly?
4. Who else is involved in the decision?
5. What's your target start and completion?
6. Have you set a budget range?

**Explain our approach:**
"We scope it properly first, give you a clear written proposal, assign one project manager, send you a weekly update, and approve any changes in writing before they happen. We're not the cheapest option by design — clients choose us for certainty and quality."

**Close:**
"Based on what you've told me, I'd suggest a site visit on [date]. After that, we'll send a written scope for your confirmation before we price anything. Does that work for you?"

**Score the client** using SOP 02 immediately after the call.
