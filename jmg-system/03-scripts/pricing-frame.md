# Holding the Pricing Frame

We are not the cheapest by design. Our clients hire us because they want the work done properly — fewer mistakes, less stress, better coordination, stronger long-term value.

## When asked "How much per square meter?"
"Every project is different, so I don't quote a number before we've defined the scope. Let me understand what you want to build and I'll give you a clear, itemized proposal. That way you know exactly what you're getting."

## When told "Another contractor is cheaper."
"That's fair, and I'd encourage you to compare carefully — scope, materials, schedule, who manages the project, and what's covered if something goes wrong. We're not the lowest price by design. Clients who work with us want certainty and quality workmanship. If that's what you're looking for, we'd be glad to build it for you."

## When asked for a discount
"We price the work to deliver it properly, so we don't discount the standard. What I can do is look at options — phasing the work or adjusting specifications — so the project fits your budget without compromising the quality."

## When the client goes quiet after the proposal
"Hi [Name], just checking in on the proposal. Happy to walk through any part of it or adjust the scope. If the timing isn't right, no problem — we'll be here when you're ready."

## Principles
- Never apologize for price.
- Never justify defensively. State the value and let it stand.
- Offer scope/spec/phasing options — never a cut in quality or safety.
