# SOP 06 — Field Execution

**Goal:** Build to spec, on schedule, with a clean and safe site and a client who always knows where things stand.
**Owner:** Project Manager + Foreman **Exit gate:** Milestones met; weekly updates on time; no open safety items.

## Daily (Foreman)
1. Morning toolbox talk: today's tasks, safety, deliveries.
2. Check quality against drawings and specs.
3. **Photograph hidden work before it is covered.**
4. Complete the **Daily Log** (`02-templates/daily-log.md`).
5. End-of-day cleanup; secure site and materials.

## Weekly (Project Manager)
1. Site walk and quality review.
2. Update schedule and budget tracker.
3. Send the **Weekly Client Update** (`02-templates/weekly-client-update.md`) on the same day each week.
4. Confirm next week's deliveries and subcontractor schedule.

## Change orders
1. Any change in scope, spec, or schedule → written **Change Order** (`02-templates/change-order.md`).
2. State cost and time impact. Client signs **before** work proceeds.
3. No signature, no work.

## Issues and delays
- Escalate to PM same day, to Owner within 24 hours if cost, schedule, or relationship is affected.
- Communicate to the client early, calmly, with the plan to resolve — never defensively.

## Milestone inspections
Rough-in, waterproofing, structural, pre-finish, and final. Each signed off before the next phase.
