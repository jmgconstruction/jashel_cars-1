# SOP 03 — Site Visit & Scope Definition

**Goal:** Replace assumptions with documented facts so the proposal is accurate and the project is predictable.
**Owner:** Estimator / Owner **Exit gate:** Written scope, inclusions, exclusions, and assumptions approved by the client.

## Before the visit
- Confirm date, access, and decision-makers present.
- Prepare: measuring tools, camera, moisture meter (if applicable), checklist.

## During the visit
1. Measure and photograph every area (wide + detail shots; label by room/area).
2. Inspect existing conditions: structure, roof, drainage, plumbing, electrical, moisture, access, utilities.
3. Note constraints: neighbors, HOA/permit requirements, delivery access, working hours.
4. Discuss the client's priorities: function, finish level, must-haves vs. nice-to-haves.
5. Confirm budget range and timeline against scope.

## After the visit (within 48 hours)
1. Write the **Scope Summary**: what is included, what is excluded, key assumptions.
2. Flag risks and unknowns (hidden conditions, allowances, permit timelines).
3. Prepare BOQ / quantities. Use unit rates from the current price book.
4. Send the Scope Summary to the client for confirmation **before** pricing.

## Rule
Scope confirmed in writing → then price. Never the reverse.
