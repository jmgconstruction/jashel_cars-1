# SOP 07 — Closeout & Referral

**Goal:** Finish properly, hand over with confidence, and turn a completed project into trust and referrals.
**Owner:** PM / Owner **Exit gate:** Punch list cleared; final walkthrough signed; review requested.

## Steps
1. **Internal walkthrough** by PM before the client sees the work.
2. Build the **Punch List** (`02-templates/punch-list.md`); assign owners and due dates; target completion within 7 days.
3. **Final client walkthrough:** present the work, explain care and maintenance, collect sign-off.
4. **Handover package:** as-built notes, warranty terms, product manuals, finish schedule, contact for service.
5. **Final invoice** and payment collected.
6. **Professional photography** of completed work (before/after where available) for portfolio.
7. **Closeout call** (10 min): ask what went well and what we can improve; log score in tracker.
8. **Referral and review request** (`03-scripts/referral-request.md`) within 7 days.
9. **Follow-ups:** 30-day and 90-day check-ins; log any warranty items.
10. **Post-project review** (internal, 30 min): schedule vs. actual, budget vs. actual, lessons learned; update price book and SOPs.
