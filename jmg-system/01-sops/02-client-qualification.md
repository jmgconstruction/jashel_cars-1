# SOP 02 — Client Qualification

**Goal:** Work with clients who value quality, process, and accountability. Protect our standards.
**Owner:** Owner **Exit gate:** Score recorded. ≥ 7/10 proceeds; < 7 is declined respectfully or referred.

## Scorecard (score each 0–2)

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|-----------|---|---|---|
| **Values quality over price** | Asks only "how much per sqm?" | Mixed priorities | Asks about process, materials, workmanship |
| **Budget realism** | Budget far below scope | Slightly under | Realistic and open about range |
| **Decision clarity** | Unclear decision-makers | Some alignment | One clear decision-maker, aligned |
| **Timeline realism** | Unrealistic or "yesterday" | Tight but workable | Realistic and flexible |
| **Respect for process** | Wants verbal deals, skips contract | Hesitant | Comfortable with written scope, contract, deposit |

**Total /10.**
- **8–10:** Priority. Schedule site visit.
- **7:** Proceed with clear expectations set up front.
- **0–6:** Decline respectfully (see `03-scripts/respectful-decline.md`) or offer a consultation-only engagement.

## Red flags
- Compares us only against the cheapest bidder
- Wants the price before the scope
- Pressures for discounts before any work is defined
- Resists written agreements or deposits
- Multiple unaligned decision-makers

## Rule
We do not chase every project. Declining the wrong client is how we keep capacity for the right ones.
