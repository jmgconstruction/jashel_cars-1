# SOP 04 — Proposal & Contract

**Goal:** Present the work as certainty, not as a price list.
**Owner:** Owner **Exit gate:** Signed contract and deposit received.

## Proposal structure (`02-templates/proposal-template.md`)
1. Understanding of the project and client priorities
2. Our approach and process (what the client can expect each week)
3. Scope: inclusions, exclusions, assumptions
4. Schedule and milestones
5. Investment (price) with payment schedule
6. Allowances and how variations are handled
7. Warranty and accountability
8. Next steps

## Presenting the proposal
- Present live (call or meeting) — never just email a PDF.
- Lead with scope and process, then investment.
- Hold the frame: "We're not the cheapest by design. Our clients choose us for the work done properly, with fewer mistakes and less stress."
- If price pushback: restate value and risk reduction; offer scope/phasing/spec options. **Do not discount for the sake of winning.**

## Contract requirements
- Signed scope, price, payment schedule, schedule, change-order process, warranty
- Deposit received before mobilization
- Insurance and permit responsibilities defined

## Follow-up
Day 2 check-in, day 5 decision call. If no decision by day 10, close politely.
