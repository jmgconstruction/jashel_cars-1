# SOP 01 — Lead Intake

**Goal:** Respond fast, look organized from the first touch, and capture what we need to qualify.
**Owner:** Admin / Owner **Exit gate:** Lead logged in tracker; first response sent within 24 hours.

## Steps
1. Log the lead in `project-tracker.csv` (source, name, contact, location, project type, date).
2. Send the **first response** (see `03-scripts/first-response.md`) within one business day.
3. Ask the intake questions:
   - What are you planning to build or renovate, and where?
   - Do you have plans or drawings, or do you need design support?
   - What is your target timeline?
   - Have you set a budget range for this project?
   - Who makes the final decision?
4. Offer a short discovery call (15–20 min). Do not quote over message.
5. Move the lead to **Qualification** (SOP 02).

## Rules
- Never give a price from a one-line message.
- Never chase. One follow-up at 3 days, one at 7 days, then close the lead politely.
