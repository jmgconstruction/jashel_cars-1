# Operating Rules — Standards That Don't Bend

1. **Scope before price.** No number goes out until the scope, inclusions, exclusions, and assumptions are written down.
2. **Nothing verbal.** Changes, approvals, and selections are confirmed in writing (message or signed change order) before work proceeds.
3. **No work without a signed contract and deposit.**
4. **We qualify clients.** We choose projects that fit our standards. We decline respectfully when price is the only criterion.
5. **We hold the pricing frame.** We do not discount to win. We adjust scope, phasing, or specification — never quality or safety.
6. **One point of contact.** Every client has a named Project Manager and a weekly update, same day each week.
7. **Plan the work, work the plan.** Pre-construction checklist is complete before mobilization.
8. **Safety and site order are non-negotiable.** Clean site at end of every day; PPE required.
9. **Inspect before covering.** Hidden work (rebar, plumbing, electrical, waterproofing) is photographed and checked before it is closed up.
10. **Respond within one business day.** Calm, clear, and direct — never defensive, never needy.
11. **Close properly.** Punch list, final walkthrough, handover documents, warranty terms, then referral request.

## Tone standard (all communication)
Friendly, trusted, calmly confident, decisive, professional, premium. Not needy, defensive, overly apologetic, salesy, or discount-focused. We sound like a dependable project leader, not a pushy salesman.
